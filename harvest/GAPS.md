## Filler harvest

`catalog/filler.json.gz` holds 4,521 short items (about 345 hours): promos 1,521, bumpers and idents 1,362, PSAs 419, news breaks and opens 371, station IDs 216, infomercials 143, cable-system filler 117, short features 102, kids-channel interstitials 62, sign-ons and sign-offs 61, telethons 52, holiday greetings 28, technical-difficulties cards 26, movie-channel intros 26, weather captures 13, emergency tests 2.

About 925 of these also sit in the commercial list and should be re-tagged there so a promo is not scheduled as an ad.

Roughly 600 items titled like "2002 Nickelodeon bumper (11)" or "2000 promo for ..." come from a few uploaders whose other uploads are Australian; treat them as doubtful until checked.

1. Network morning shows: 42 on site, almost none before 2000. Archive has roughly 30 a band by title; most of the rest sit inside long home tapes.
2. Station sign-on, sign-off, anthem and test pattern: the site draws its own bars. 61 real ones harvested, strongest 1984-95.
3. Promos and bumpers between shows: none scheduled today. 2,883 harvested, every year covered.
4. PSAs: none scheduled. 419 harvested.
5. Overnight paid programming: 3 on site. 143 harvested, half-hour infomercials mostly 1992 on.
6. Religious and televangelist blocks: 15 on site, none after 1991. Archive is thin from 1988-99 and decent only in 1984-87 and 2000-04.
7. Home shopping: none on site. Archive holds about 27 by title, nearly all 1992 on.
8. Weather channel: 20 on site. Archive holds about 40; 13 more captures in the filler set.
9. Classic reruns as aired: 53 on site against a channel that needs hundreds. Archive is thin before 1996 and better after.
10. Cooking and home shows: 77 on site, with home shows empty after 1999. Archive holds about 50 cooking and 85 home by title.
11. Court shows: 13 on site, none 1992-95. Archive has about 20 before 2000 and 69 after.
12. Outdoors, car, travel and exercise shows: 33 on site combined. Archive has about 100 car, 90 exercise, 40 travel and only 11 fishing or hunting.
13. Country and Nashville programming: 56 on site for a full-time country channel. Archive shows about 340, with a spike in 1996-99.
14. Soaps on NBC and CBS: the ABC soaps dominate 1992-93 and most other years have under 20 across all three networks. Archive has about 95 NBC and 70 CBS by title.
15. Talk and entertainment magazines: talk has 2 to 5 items a year in 1984-86 and 2002-04; magazines under 10 a year after 1994. Archive shows about 300 talk and 570 magazine hits, so both can be filled.

Also thin: pageants (8), telethons (0 on site, 52 harvested), high school and filler sports (22), rodeo and monster trucks (10), BET-style shows (9), telecourses (0; archive about 145), late night before 1989 (under 26 a year), wrestling before 1988 (under 45 a year), sports talk and highlight shows (10).
